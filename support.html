Color Hunt Together Support

Need help with your account, a purchase, gameplay, privacy, or safety? Email colorhunttogether@gmail.com. Please include your in-app username, device platform, app version, and a short description of what happened. Never send your password.

Purchases and subscriptions

Use Settings → Restore Purchases if a purchase or Premium Hunter subscription is missing after reinstalling or changing devices. Subscription billing and cancellation are managed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Include the store receipt or order identifier when contacting support, but do not send payment-card details.

Report content or another player

Use the report option on a post, hunt, comment, or profile. You can also block a player from their profile. For urgent safety or abuse concerns, email support with the username and relevant content details.

Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account and associated content from Settings → Advanced → Delete Account. You can also review the account deletion instructions. Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately.

Response time

We aim to respond to support and safety reports as quickly as possible. Complex purchase, deletion, or moderation requests may require additional verification.
