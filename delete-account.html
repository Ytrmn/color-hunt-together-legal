Delete Your Account

Account deletion is available directly inside the Color Hunt Together app.

  1. Open the Color Hunt Together app and sign in.
  2. Go to Options → Advanced.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

You can also request deletion without the app by emailing colorhunttogether@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account. Include your username so we can identify the correct account.

Deletion permanently removes your account and associated profile, hunts, photos, posts, messages, social connections, and gameplay data. It cannot be undone. Information that must be retained for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance may be kept only as required.

If you have an active subscription, deleting your account does not cancel billing through Apple or Google. Cancel it first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Open the app

If the button above doesn't open the app, make sure Color Hunt Together is installed, then follow the steps manually from the Options menu.
